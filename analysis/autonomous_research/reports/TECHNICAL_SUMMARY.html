Possession classification for tactical football match frames

MTAH 2027 technical summary. Shipped system final_v5.

1. Method

Each frame gets home, away, dead ball, or abstain, ranked by spellF1 (temporal IoU). A histogram-gradient-boosted classifier reads frame features, trained on nine external matches with event/rule-derived labels, degraded to tactical-view noise, with mirrors and noise draws. A fixed-cost Viterbi decoder enforces switch and dead costs, minimum dead length, half kickoff, and stop gate.

5. Limitations

  • Public 17 possessions; no statistical significance established.
  • Nine external matches reused; Game 3 holdout consumed.
  • Ball missing on about 41 percent of graded frames.
  • No explicit goal-event tags; formation mode priors already tested negative.
  • Paused after tested fixed-variant physical latch fails. Not all physical methods ruled out.
