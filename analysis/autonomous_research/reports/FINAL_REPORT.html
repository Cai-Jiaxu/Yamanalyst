Possession classification for tactical match frames

MTAH 2027 technical report. Aggregate metrics only. No match video or frame-level data is included.
Result. The shipped system final_v5 scores public spellF1 0.7226 and macroF1 0.9098. The extension work summarized here tested several further decoder and training ideas. None earned promotion. DoG dev bench +0.006 but full public 0.6857 vs 0.7226.

1. What the system does

noised external tracking→ frame features (shape, distance, ball)→ gradient-boosted classifier→ Viterbi temporal decoder→ possession spells

Each of 199,319 tactical frames is assigned home, away, dead ball, or abstain. The ranking metric is spellF1, a temporal IoU over predicted possessions. The classifier is trained on nine external matches with event-derived labels provided by the source dataset, degraded to the tactical-view noise model, with left-right and home-away mirrors and three extra noise draws. A fixed-cost Viterbi decoder enforces team-switch and dead-ball costs, a minimum dead-ball length, a half kickoff reset, and a stoppage gate.

4. Data and labels

  • Training uses event-derived labels from nine external matches. Whether their possession convention differs from the target task is an open hypothesis.
  • The public 17 possessions and the ball-mask yardstick are never used to train the model. They are reported after each run. Selecting ideas against them is development selection, not independent validation.
  • A third Metrica sample match was used as a one-time external holdout. E1 and the baseline tied on spellF1 (0.6128), with a hair of macroF1 in E1's favour. Descriptive only, no confidence interval. It shares the Metrica label convention and is not the full fixture ground truth.
  • No hand-labelled frames from the target match are created or used. The system abstains on pre-match, halftime and post-whistle frames.

6. Assumptions and limitations

  • Public labels are 17 possessions. No confidence interval or statistical significance established; all yardsticks reused are development.
  • Nine external matches span two datasets. The fresh Metrica holdout is limited to one match and the same label convention.
  • The ball is missing on about 41 percent of graded frames; ball reconstruction was measured and adds little.
  • Repeated development use of the same yardsticks biases small upward moves. The shipped numbers are the measured ones, not a private-match estimate.

7. Reproduction

The shipped model is deterministic. The submission final_v5 has sha256 4089da23...1c8a, 199,319 rows. The official scorer gives spellF1 0.7226 and macroF1 0.9098 over 17 graded possessions. No cloud service or external weight is used at inference.

Reproduce with the project venv:

.venv/bin/python evaluation/run_evaluation.py --results evaluation/results --gt public
The scorer reads a directory of submissions and writes a scoreboard.
