4. Validation caveats (read before trusting any number)

yardsticktruth sourceindependencecaveat
public organiser public GT, frames 11185..18684, 17 possessions worked example, never tune ball visible 88-99% here, vs 59% match-wide. Best-tracked window.
masked same public GT, ball hidden in 4-40 s clumps, 16 seeds not independent reuses the same 17 possessions. sd 0.024 is ball-placement noise, not possession variance.
ext IDSSE 7 Bundesliga + Metrica 1/2, true per-frame labels other matches shifted label conventions and source domain (no tactical camera, ATD degraded). Cross-match transfer, not in-domain.
val_h2 4 hand-labelled late-2H windows, 57 spells in-domain, hand labels eye labels; timing convention was shown to reward rule boundaries. Allowed for validation only.
private full-match corrected tracking, 158,857 graded frames final invisible until submission. One of several generalization surfaces, not the only one.
Adoption gate is a development practical criterion, not a significance test. A new feature is promoted only if bench rises by at least 0.005 vs 0.6612, ext does not fall by more than 0.005 vs 0.6163, masked does not fall by more than 0.005 vs 0.7061, and public sanity drop is under 0.02. Differences under 0.005 are noise. This gate was calibrated over dozens of internal variants. It does not guarantee a private-match improvement.
